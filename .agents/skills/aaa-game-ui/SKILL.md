# aaa-game-ui

## Workflow

Read existing layout and frontend-design skill. Preserve black, crimson and antique gold visual language, ruined castle, original portraits and cards. Prioritize readable hierarchy, deliberate typography, restrained metallic ornament, clear active captain and draft state. Avoid generic dashboard cards, excessive glow and replacing approved assets. Check landscape and portrait layouts, touch targets, clipping, contrast and reduced motion. Capture before/after screenshots at representative sizes; do not claim AAA quality solely from code.

## Project constraints

Work in the existing index.html, code/ and media/ structure. Keep the game local and offline; no server, public hosting or online mode. Do not change unrelated mechanics or replace approved assets without explicit approval. Use a scoped diff and document tests and limitations. Coordinate with game-audit after implementation.
