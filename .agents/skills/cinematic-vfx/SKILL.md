# cinematic-vfx

## Workflow

Inspect existing scene markup, CSS, JS and media before editing. Preserve approved artwork and character identity. Prefer original footage or textured layered assets over cartoon procedural shapes. Design anticipation, impact, decay and transition with explicit timing and z-order. Use transform/opacity where possible, bounded particles, and stop hidden effects. Keep state transitions independent of animation success; provide cancellation and reduced-motion fallback. Compare before/after screenshots and test interruption, repeated activation and offline playback.

## Project constraints

Work in the existing index.html, code/ and media/ structure. Keep the game local and offline; no server, public hosting or online mode. Do not change unrelated mechanics or replace approved assets without explicit approval. Use a scoped diff and document tests and limitations. Coordinate with game-audit after implementation.
