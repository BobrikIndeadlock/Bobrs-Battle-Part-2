# gothic-environment-animation

## Workflow

Inspect current intro, throne, living-throne, cinematic-throne and spirits styles/scripts and original media. Establish foreground, midground and background layers with stable perspective. Animate subtle candle and brazier light, drifting volumetric-looking fog, embers, banners and moonlight only where consistent with approved art. Keep architecture stable; avoid warping faces, artificial neon, cartoon particles and excessive camera shake. Anchor Makarych spirits above his own pedestal with staggered emergence and smooth fade; do not restore removed Artemon bats without approval. Preserve grimoire and card hit areas, captain visibility and readable UI. Prefer existing realistic footage/textures; request new media when procedural effects cannot meet the visual target. Use bounded loops, transform/opacity, visibility-based pausing and reduced-motion alternatives. Test screenshots, full transitions, offline operation and mobile performance; report actual measurements and limitations.

## Project constraints

Work in the existing index.html, code/ and media/ structure. Keep the game local and offline; no server, public hosting or online mode. Do not change unrelated mechanics or replace approved assets without explicit approval. Use a scoped diff and document tests and limitations. Coordinate with game-audit after implementation.
